Yellowing soybean leaves in July usually have one of three causes. Waterlogging after heavy rain starves roots of oxygen, so the whole plant turns pale; improving drainage channels is the first fix. Nitrogen deficiency shows as uniform yellowing of older leaves first, often where nodulation is poor. Yellow mosaic virus, spread by whiteflies, causes bright yellow patches mixed with green and spreads in clusters. In our field notes from Latur and Osmanabad this season, waterlogging explained most cases reported in the first two weeks after the heavy spell. Pull up a plant and check the roots: healthy nodules are pink inside.
