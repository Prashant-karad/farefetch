Onions can lose a large share of their weight and value in storage through rotting, sprouting and moisture loss. Proper curing in the field for a few days after harvest, until the neck is dry, is the most important step. Store onions in well-ventilated structures with a raised slatted floor so air moves underneath. Avoid storing in heaps on the ground, which traps heat and moisture. Sort out damaged and thick-necked bulbs before storage, because they rot first and spread infection. Low-cost ventilated storage structures supported under state schemes have helped farmers in Nashik hold stock longer and sell when prices improve.
