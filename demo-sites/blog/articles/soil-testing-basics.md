Soil testing tells a farmer what the field actually needs. Collect soil from several places across the field in a zigzag pattern, at a depth of about fifteen centimetres. Mix the samples well in a clean bucket and take around half a kilo for the lab. Avoid sampling near bunds, trees, or where manure was recently dumped. Do the test a few weeks before sowing so the report arrives in time to plan fertilizer. A typical report covers nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium, pH and organic carbon. Following the recommended doses usually lowers fertilizer cost and avoids damage from overuse.
