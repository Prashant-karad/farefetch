Drip irrigation has a high upfront cost but pays back through water and labour savings. The main costs are the main and sub-main pipes, laterals with drippers, a filter unit, and a fertigation tank. Government subsidy schemes cover a large share of the cost for small and marginal farmers, so check eligibility before buying. Farmers we spoke with growing pomegranate and sugarcane reported the biggest savings, because these crops need water over a long season. Maintenance matters: clogged drippers are the most common reason systems fail within three years. Flush laterals regularly and clean filters every week during heavy use.
