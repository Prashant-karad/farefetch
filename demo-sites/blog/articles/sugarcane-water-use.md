Sugarcane is one of the most water-intensive crops in Maharashtra. Furrow irrigation wastes a lot of water through runoff and deep percolation. Switching to drip irrigation, alternate furrow irrigation, or trash mulching can cut water use significantly. Trash mulching means spreading the dry leaves from the previous crop between rows instead of burning them; it reduces evaporation and adds organic matter. Ratoon management, meaning growing a second crop from the stubble, also saves water and cost compared to replanting. Farmers should match irrigation to crop stage, since the grand growth phase needs far more water than the early or ripening stages.
