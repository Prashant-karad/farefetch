Compound interest means you earn interest on your interest. Put 1,000 dollars away at 7 percent a year and you have 1,070 after one year, about 1,967 after ten years and about 7,612 after thirty years, without adding anything. Starting early matters more than starting big, because time does most of the work. The same maths works against you on credit card debt.
