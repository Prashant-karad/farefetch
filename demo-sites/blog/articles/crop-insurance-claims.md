Many crop insurance claims are delayed or rejected because of avoidable mistakes. For localized losses like hailstorm or inundation, the loss usually must be reported within a short window after the event, so report quickly through the official app, helpline or your bank. Keep photos of the damaged crop with the date visible, and keep your sowing records ready. Make sure the crop and area in your insurance record match what you actually sowed, since mismatches are a common reason for rejection. Follow up with the insurance company and the agriculture office, and keep the complaint or docket number for every contact.
