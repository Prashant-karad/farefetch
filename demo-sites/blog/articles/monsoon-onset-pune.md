For ten seasons our volunteers in Pune district recorded the first day of steady monsoon rain at twelve village rain gauges. In our log, onset dates varied by more than three weeks between years, and in several years villages only twenty kilometres apart saw onset a week apart. The practical lesson from our data is that sowing by calendar date is risky. Waiting for cumulative rainfall of around 75 to 100 millimetres, enough to wet the soil to sowing depth, reduced failed germination in our volunteers' plots. Our complete village-level table, with daily totals for the first month of each season, is available to subscribers.
