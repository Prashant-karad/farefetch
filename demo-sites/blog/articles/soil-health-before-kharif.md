Before the kharif season, a simple soil check saves money on fertilizer. Farmers in our Pune district field visits who tested nitrogen, phosphorus and potassium before sowing applied fertilizer in measured doses instead of guessing. The most common gap we saw was low organic carbon in fields that had been under continuous soybean and wheat rotation. Adding farmyard manure or green manure crops like sunhemp before sowing improved soil structure and water holding. Testing should be done at least three weeks before sowing so results arrive in time. Take samples from several spots in a zigzag pattern across the field, mix them, and send about half a kilo to the nearest soil testing lab.
