Keep it to one page if you have less than ten years of experience. Lead each bullet with a result, not a task: 'cut page load time by 40 percent' beats 'worked on performance'. Match the words in the job description so screening software finds you. Put projects with links near the top if you are early in your career. Remove photos, full addresses and long objective statements.
