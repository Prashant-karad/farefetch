Testing soil shows what your field really needs. Take soil from several spots across the field in a zigzag line, about fifteen centimetres deep. Mix everything in a clean bucket and keep roughly half a kilo for the laboratory. Do not sample near bunds, trees, or fresh manure heaps. Test a few weeks before sowing so the results come back in time to plan fertilizer use. Most reports include nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium, pH and organic carbon. Using the recommended amounts generally cuts fertilizer spending and prevents harm from applying too much.
