Week one: review arrays, strings and hash maps, and solve two easy problems a day. Week two: move to linked lists, stacks, queues and trees. Week three: practise graphs, recursion and dynamic programming with one medium problem a day. Week four: do timed mock interviews and practise explaining your thinking out loud. Interviewers care about how you reason, not only the final answer, so talk through trade-offs and test your code with small examples.
