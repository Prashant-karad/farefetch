We forget most new information within days unless we review it. Spaced repetition schedules reviews just before you would forget: after one day, then three days, then a week, then a month. Each successful review pushes the next one further away. Flashcard apps automate the schedule. Ten minutes of spaced review a day beats hours of rereading notes the night before a test.
